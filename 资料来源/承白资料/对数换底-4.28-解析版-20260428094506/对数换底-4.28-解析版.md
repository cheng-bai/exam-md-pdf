设 $a, b, c, d$ 均为正数，且 $a, c$ 均不为 1 . 求证: ${\log }_{a}b \cdot  {\log }_{c}d = {\log }_{a}d \cdot  {\log }_{c}b$ .

## 答案

证明见解析

解析

运用换底公式证明即可.

由题意,根据换底公式, ${\log }_{a}b \cdot  {\log }_{c}d = \frac{\ln b}{\ln a} \times  \frac{\ln d}{\ln c} = \frac{\ln d}{\ln a} \times  \frac{\ln b}{\ln c} = {\log }_{a}d \cdot  {\log }_{c}b$ ,命题得证.
